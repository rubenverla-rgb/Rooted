How it works

Three steps to new roots.

1

Take a cutting

Cut a healthy shoot just below a node. That is where the roots will grow from.

2

Place it in water

Fill the glass tube, add your cutting and put the cork stopper on top. Ready to grow.

3

Watch them grow

Refresh the water now and then and enjoy the roots slowly filling the tubes.
